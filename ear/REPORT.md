# Ear training report

Generated by `python -m shruti train --captures 150 --seed 1` on simulated buses (ear/protosim.py). Every number is for the exact integer model (ear/mlp.py) that the hardware runs, on captures held out of training. Simulated data only: FPGA captures of real devices come later. The sections from "Coverage by speed" on are generated by `python -m ear.evaluate --write` (ear/evaluate.py) and use the same exact integer model.
